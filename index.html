About CarePlus Clinic

A Modern Multispeciality Medical Home in Hubballi

CarePlus Multispeciality Clinic was established with a singular mission: to provide families in Hubballi-Dharwad with high-quality, comprehensive clinical care in a calm, modern setting.

Unlike overcrowded hospital OPDs, our clinic focuses on unhurried consultations, digital appointment schedules, and coordinated multispeciality care under one roof. Whether you need an experienced family physician for chronic diabetes management, a gentle pediatrician for newborn vaccinations, or clinical dermatological care, our doctors are here to listen.
